# Federal marginal brackets (planning)

Educational stub for Lab RAG — **not tax advice**.

- A marginal bracket is the rate applied to the next dollar of taxable income, not the average rate on all income.
- ECE estimates brackets after subtracting standard deduction and optional pre-tax deferrals (401k/HSA) on the paystub vision path.
- Filing status (Single, MFJ, MFS, HoH) changes bracket thresholds.
- Do not use Lab answers as a substitute for a CPA or tax software.
