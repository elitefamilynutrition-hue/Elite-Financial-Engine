# Paystub pre-tax deferrals

Educational stub for Lab RAG — **not tax advice**.

- Traditional 401(k) and HSA employee contributions typically reduce taxable wages for federal income tax purposes (subject to limits and plan rules).
- ECE annualizes period deductions from OCR'd paystubs when updating `marginal_tax_bracket`.
- Roth deferrals do not reduce current taxable income the same way.
- Always confirm plan documents and IRS contribution limits for the calendar year.
